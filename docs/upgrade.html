When block keepers can switch

Every block keeper has its own epoch of about 24 h, and the ends are spread over the whole day. A keeper can only switch to the new version at the end of its epoch. The chart shows how many block keeper epochs end in each hour from now.
Not the same as the mining epoch (5.5 min) or the 24-h mining day with the daily tap limit – those run on their own clock.

Live data from https://mainnet-cf.ackinacki.org/v2/bk_set_update, refreshed every 60 s. Block time is estimated from two readings. Field protocol_version_support: A = runs A; A->B = runs A and supports B for the switch. Unofficial helper page by NeoRider.
